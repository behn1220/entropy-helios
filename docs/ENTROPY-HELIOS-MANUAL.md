# ENTROPY HELIOS (v1.0 Beta) — User & Studio Manual

**Standalone Dual-Core 8-Voice Ambient Drone & Microtonal Synthesizer for the M-VAVE FM-1**

---

## 1. Quick Start (First 60 Seconds)

1. **Flash Firmware:** Open `entropy-helios-1.0.html` in Chrome or Edge, connect your FM-1 over USB-C, and click **Install**. After reboot, confirm **`HELIOS 1.0`** in the top-left corner of the screen.
2. **Start Listening:** Turn up the **MASTER** volume knob. You'll start in **`GEN DRONE`** mode on Patch **`P01 WARM ANALOG SEA`**.
3. **Browse 36 Factory Presets (Grouped by Mood) + 8 User Slots:** Turn **`PRESET`**:
   - **`P01–P20 [CALM]`** — 20 warm analog ambient presets grouped together (*P01–P16 lush evolving ambient classics • P17–P20 ultra-warm sub-heavy analog bliss*). All 20 automatically engage **Analog Warmth Guard** during evolution.
   - **`P21–P28 [LUSH]`** — 8 cinematic synth & sequencer patches (*Neon Rain 82, Desert Wind, Helios Corona, Cathedral VCF, Berlin Sequencer, Glass Nebula, Matrix Voyager, Symbiosis Dawn*).
   - **`P29–P36 [EDGE]`** — 8 experimental industrial, Geiger, ring-mod, and dark drone patches.
   - **`U01–U08 [USER]`** — 8 permanent flash slots for your own patches (saves chassis colorway, stacked FX cartridges, per-cartridge mix/tone, pin matrix, and all knobs).
4. **Pick Your Chassis Finish:** Press **`GLO`** and turn **`K1 (CHASSIS)`** to switch live between 5 anodized finishes (**`BLACK`**, **`CREAM`**, **`AMBER YEL`**, **`COBALT`**, **`OLIVE`**). Your choice saves automatically to flash!
5. **Zoom Into Any Module:** Look at the tiny keycap badges (**`EDT`**, **`SEQ`**, **`FX`**, **`ARP`**) on `HOME`—press any module button to smoothly expand it across 80% of the screen with giant 3D color-coded knobs. Press the same button at the end of its cycle (or press **`HOME`**) to zoom back out!

## 4. Stackable 16-Cartridge FX Rack (`FX` → `FX`)

You can **stack any combination of the 16 FX cartridges simultaneously** with independent level and tone per cartridge:
1. Press **`FX`** twice to zoom into **`FX CARTRIDGE`**.
2. Turn **`K1 (CART SEL)`** to browse all **16 cartridges**:
   `1. SHIMMER HALL` • `2. SPACE ECHO` • `3. BLACKHOLE` • `4. GRANULAR` • `5. PHASER+REV` • `6. SUB VOID` • `7. REVERSE TAPE` • `8. RINGMOD HALL` • `9. LO-FI CRUSH` • `10. CHORUS+PITCH` • `11. SPECTRAL RES` • `12. INF FREEZE` • `13. VIBROTREM` • `14. AUTO-WAH` • `15. BELL PITCH` • `16. TAPE WASH`
3. Turn **`K2 (CART ON)`** clockwise (`LOADED`) to stack it into the chain, or counter-clockwise (`OFF`) to unload it.
4. Dial in **`K3 (CART LVL)`** and **`K4 (CART TON)`** independently for each stacked cartridge.
5. Press **`REC`** on `FX CART` at any time to **solo** the currently selected cartridge (clearing all other stacked carts).

## 5. Performance Modes, USB Audio FX & Studio Companion

- **3 Performance Modes (`REC` or `SEL + ALGO`):**
  - **`GEN DRONE`**: Autonomous generative 6-voice drones + dual 5-step sequencers + 12 copper microtonal touch plates.
  - **`HYBRID`**: Left hand (`F3–E4`) directs drone chord root/voicing; right hand (`F4–G5`) plays expressive velocity/aftertouch lead & arpeggiator.
  - **`PLAY 8V`**: Full 27-key 8-voice polyphonic synthesizer with velocity pluck and per-key polyphonic aftertouch!
- **Live USB Audio In → Helios FX Engine:** Connect the FM-1 to a PC/Mac/iPad/phone, send stereo audio to the `ENTROPY HELIOS FM-1` USB Audio device, and on **`ENV`** set **`SEL* K1 (EXT SRC)` = `USB AUDIO`** and turn up **`K1 (EXT IN)`** to process external audio through the dual analog VCFs, 3-Spring Tank, and 16 stacked FX cartridges!
- **Studio Recording (`GLO → SEL* → K4`):** Set **`USB REC`** to **`0dB FULL`** (unity digital output independent of Master volume) or **`SPK MUTE`** (mutes internal speaker for silent recording).
- **WebMIDI Studio & Patch Librarian:** Open `entropy-helios-studio.html` in Chrome/Edge while connected over USB-C for a live 8×8 pin matrix editor, 2D mouse/touch joystick, 16-cartridge selector, and JSON backup/restore of User Slots `U01–U08`.
- **External MIDI CC Map:** `CC 16/17` Joy X/Y • `CC 74/75` VCF Cut L/R • `CC 71` VCF Res • `CC 72` Drive • `CC 80/81` Spring Mix/Decay • `CC 82` Spring Kick • `CC 83` Freeze • `CC 84` Cartridge • `CC 85` A/B Morph • `CC 91/93` Cart Mix/Shimmer • `CC 64` Sustain • External MIDI Clock (`0xF8`/`0xFA`/`0xFC`) auto-locks BPM.

## 6. Troubleshooting & Reset

- **Audio sounds too dark/bright or wild after tweaking:** Short-press **`SAVE`** to cleanly reload the current preset.
- **Want to wipe all user slots & settings:** Hold **`SAVE`** for **3 full seconds** for a complete Factory Reset.
- **Check running version:** Look at the top-left corner of the FM-1 screen (**`HELIOS 1.0`**).
